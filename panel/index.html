— Pressroom notes —

The wire runs
at four critics,
one masthead.

The side panel is the fixture. Everything else — the settings drawer, the onboarding wizard, the tap-menu on a reaction — decorates around it. The waveform is a printed receipt: when it jumps, the feed is hot; when it flattens, the stage is dark.

Three rules for the wire: one channel per role, one tag per line, one reaction per beat. No threaded replies. No emoji. If a critic needs more than a sentence, send them to the back.

Every persona is an AI fan parody of a public figure — text only, never a voice clone or image likeness, with a standing disclosure baked into every prompt. The masthead wears that framing in ink.

Tag legend

FactCitation + correction. Baba Booey (Howard) or Molly Wood (TWiST).
DunkThe one-liner. Short, mean, quotable.
CueSFX + stage direction. No words needed.
BitThe premise that becomes a running joke.
