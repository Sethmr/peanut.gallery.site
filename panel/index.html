— Pressroom notes —

The wire runs
at four critics,
thirteen bars.

The side panel is the fixture. Everything else — the Tweaks, the pack picker, the self-host console — decorates around it. The waveform is a printed receipt: when it jumps, the feed is hot; when it flattens, the stage is dark; when it steps, you're in replay.

Three rules for the wire: one channel per role, one tag per line, one reaction per beat. No threaded replies. No emoji. If a critic needs more than a sentence, send them to the back.

Jason would have to say it qualifies — because it's not AI sludge. It's four voices, a timecode, and a waveform that moves when somebody is actually talking.

Tag legend

FactCitation + correction. Receipts attached.
DunkThe one-liner. Short, mean, quotable.
CueSFX + stage direction. No words needed.
BitThe premise that becomes a running joke.

Mug states

Speaking — this critic is composing a reaction right now.
Idle — listening, no reaction queued.
Muted — tap the mug to bring them back.
Chrome footprint
340 px
Docks to the native side panel. No floating windows, no layout shift.
Word → wire
< 300 ms
Transcript chunk to reaction, measured on the hosted backend.
Waveform
15 bars
Three accent colors: ink, stamp, highlighter. Flat = idle. Step = replay.
Channels
4 · 1
Four critics, one masthead, one drawer, one gear. Mute per critic.
