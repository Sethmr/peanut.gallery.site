How you can use the Service today

You pick at runtime in the Extension’s Backend & keys drawer:

  • Demo Mode — 15 minutes of free transcription on our API keys, one-time per install. Gated by a per-installation UUID (installId) generated locally.
  • My-Keys Mode (BYOK) — you supply your own API keys. We forward them per-request to the providers and never store them server-side. In this mode, Manu Games is not a processor on the AI-inference path: you are in direct privity with the provider whose key you supplied, under that provider’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • Self-host — deploy the MIT-licensed backend yourself. We don’t see any data in this mode; this policy doesn’t apply to your self-hosted instance.

Peanut Gallery Plus (planned future tier): not currently offered. Code paths in the repository that reference a Plus subscription, a license key of the form pg-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx, or Stripe checkout are dormant and gated off in production. If and when we launch that tier, this policy will be updated to describe the subscription-specific data flows with at least 30 days’ advance notice to any then-active subscribers.

What the Extension keeps on your device

The Extension writes to chrome.storage.local, which Chrome persists on your disk. Nothing there leaves your browser unless you trigger it:

  • Your chosen backend URL, pasted API keys (if any), persona pack, theme, response-rate dial, sensitivity level, and per-persona mute list.
  • Your per-install UUID (installId) — used to meter the Demo trial.
  • Up to 50 prior sessions’ metadata, sampled transcript tails (capped at 4,000 characters per session), and persona reactions — groundwork for a future “Past sessions” feature. LRU-evicted at 50.
  • Per-session upvotes / downvotes / pinned entry, plus UI state.

What the backend receives and keeps

The hosted backend at api.peanutgallery.live is a stateless API behind a session ID. When you stream a session it handles:

  • Audio — received as PCM, forwarded to the transcription provider with the mip_opt_out=true flag set, and discarded. Nothing is written to disk. The opt-out flag tells Deepgram not to retain or use your audio to train AI models.
  • Live transcript — held in memory during the session, routed to the Director and personas, discarded on session end.
  • Your API keys (My-Keys Mode) — forwarded per-request to the providers, redacted from every log line, never persisted.
  • Your install ID (Demo Mode) — kept in memory to meter the 15-minute trial.
  • Operational logs — request IDs, session IDs, persona IDs, response times, error events. 30-day retention. Redacted of keys; never include complete transcripts or audio.

If and when the planned Peanut Gallery Plus tier launches, additional fields (subscriber email, license key, Stripe subscription ID, weekly usage counter) will be persisted with retention windows documented here at that time.

AI training on your content

We do not train AI models on your audio, transcripts, or content, and we configure each provider to minimize retention to the extent each provider offers that control:

  • Deepgram — mip_opt_out=true is sent on every transcription request. Under Deepgram’s Model Improvement Partnership Program documentation, this limits retention to the duration necessary to process the request and excludes the content from model training.
  • Anthropic — API traffic is non-training by default per Anthropic’s commercial posture. We do not opt in to any training-partnership program.
  • xAI — Enterprise ToS §3.3 provides that xAI shall not use User Content for training and auto-deletes within 30 days absent a legal hold or safety flag.
  • Brave Search — when enabled, search queries are submitted for grounding only; we do not cache results beyond transient operational storage, consistent with Brave’s Search API Terms §3(b)(i).

These configurations protect content flowing through the Service. They do not control what you do with persona output after it leaves the Service, or what providers do under their own terms with your account in BYOK mode.

Feedback on persona reactions

When you click the menu on a persona reaction and choose upvote, downvote, pin, or quote-card, we log that action to /api/feedback. Each log entry contains the action, the persona, the pack, the reaction text, the transcript tail (~500 characters) that informed the reaction, your install ID, and a timestamp. We retain these for 90 days.

We use them to improve the personas’ prompts, the Director’s routing, and the session-recall highlight picker. Feedback is keyed to the pseudonymous install ID only — we don’t tie votes to your email or license key.

Opting out: today, either self-host the backend with DISABLE_FEEDBACK_LOGGING=true or don’t interact with the feed menu. A user-facing opt-out toggle in the Extension is on the roadmap and should ship before the first public paid launch.

Third-party providers

When the Service calls a third party on your behalf, that provider’s terms and privacy practices apply. Current subprocessors:

  • Deepgram — speech-to-text streaming.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — Producer and Joker persona inference.
  • xAI (Grok) — Troll and Sound FX persona inference, optional live search.
  • Brave Search — fact-check search queries.
  • Stripe — payment processing (Plus only). Stripe collects card details directly; we never see them.
  • Resend — transactional email (Plus only): welcome email with your license key, recovery email if you lose the key, cancellation confirmation. Email addresses are processed by Resend for delivery only; not used for marketing.
  • Railway — backend hosting.
  • Vercel — Next.js application hosting where used.
  • GitHub Pages — marketing-site hosting.
  • Google Analytics — aggregate page-view metrics on the marketing site only (not in the Extension).

Marketing-site analytics

The marketing site at www.peanutgallery.live loads Google Analytics (property G-3R9CK4LRGF) to measure aggregate page views. GA receives your IP address, user agent, referring URL, and page-view events, and sets cookies or equivalent local-storage identifiers. We use the data only in aggregate. We do not enable Google Signals, remarketing, or any advertising features. The Extension itself does not load any analytics library.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you have the right to know what we hold, correct it, delete it, port it, object to certain processing, and complain to a data-protection authority. Under U.S. state privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and equivalents) you also have the right to opt out of “sale” and “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information in the senses those laws define.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@peanutgallery.live. We may ask you to verify your identity before we act (for example, by confirming the email tied to your Stripe subscription). We respond within the timeframes required by law — typically 45 days under U.S. state privacy laws and one month under GDPR.

International data transfers

The hosted backend is in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the U.S., your audio, transcript, and (for Plus) email and license key pass through U.S.-based infrastructure. Where applicable, we rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, the U.K. International Data Transfer Addendum, and (where participating) the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. If that’s not acceptable for your situation, use My-Keys Mode or self-host the backend in a region of your choice.

Children

Peanut Gallery is not directed at children under 13 (16 in some EU member states). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under those ages. If you’re a parent and believe a child under the relevant age has subscribed or otherwise given us data, email privacy@peanutgallery.live and we’ll refund, delete, and revoke.

Security

HTTPS for all backend traffic, keys redacted from logs, Stripe handles all payment data, structured per-request timeouts on every upstream call, narrow content-script scope (peanutgallery.live subdomains only). Responsible disclosure via SECURITY.md or security@peanutgallery.live.

Recording consent

The Extension can be pointed at any browser tab you grant access to. Recording-consent laws are jurisdiction-specific. U.S. states including California, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Montana, New Hampshire, Nevada, Pennsylvania, and Washington require all-party consent for private conversations. The EU, UK, and many other jurisdictions impose equivalent or stricter rules. You are solely responsible for obtaining any consent required before directing the Extension at audio — this applies to live calls, conferences, voicemail, ambient room audio, and private streams. See the Acceptable Use section in the Terms of Service.

Changes to this policy

Material changes affecting Plus subscribers get at least 30 days’ email notice and a pro-rata refund window. Non-material changes (clarifications, typos, provider substitutions that don’t alter data handling) are effective on the updated “Last updated” date above.
