§ 01 · The free path
The simplest guide possible

Self-hosting is now the only path to run Peanut Gallery. The source is MIT-licensed — you clone it, you run it, you own it, you carry the operator responsibilities for any session you produce. Three providers, three steps, done.

1

Clone the source

git clone https://github.com/Sethmr/peanut.gallery — full stack in one repo. The self-host walkthrough covers loading the extension unpacked into Chrome.

2

Get three keys

Sign up at these three sites. Every one has a free tier that covers casual use:

Click the gear icon at the bottom of the side panel → pick My keys → paste the three strings.

§ 02 · Never shipped
What was planned

Plus never shipped. A flat-rate hosted backend (pay once, skip the API keys) was designed as an accessibility tier for non-technical users. It never launched — Manu Games LLC unpublished the Chrome Web Store listing on 2026-05-02 before any subscription opened — and it is not offered or planned. There is nothing to buy here; the payment and subscription code has been removed from the source.

The only way to run Peanut Gallery is the free path above: your own keys (BYOK) or self-host. If you fork the open-source repo and stand up your own hosted tier, you own the entire operator side — billing, taxes, ToS, abuse handling, provider-policy flow-down, and legal exposure for the sessions you produce.

§ 03 · FAQ
Is it really free?
Yes. Peanut Gallery is free and MIT-licensed — fork the repo, it's all there. Run it on your own provider keys (BYOK) or self-host. There is no paid tier and no payment processing.
Is my audio stored anywhere?
No. Audio is streamed to Deepgram live, transcripts fan out to Anthropic + xAI live, everything is held in memory for the duration of the session and discarded when you click Stop. No logs, no recordings, no analytics inside the extension. The privacy policy covers the full picture.
What models does it use?
Deepgram Nova-3 for real-time transcription, Claude Haiku for the fact-checker + comedy writer, xAI Grok 4.1 Fast for the troll + sound-effects guy.
Is this AI-generated content safe to show at work?
Sometimes. The Troll is designed to dunk and the comedy writer is designed to joke — edit the personas (the extension supports sideloading your own pack) if you want a more SFW room. The Operator's Manual covers writing your own pack.
Are these the real people?
No. The personas are generic archetypes — an unofficial, unauthorized fan parody and homage to talk- and podcast-style formats (including The Morning Crew and This Week in Startups). No license, no endorsement, no affiliation; the AI is prompted to clarify it's a fan parody if asked whether it's a real person, and it ships no verbatim copyrighted material. Full legal framing in the Terms of Service. To request a name be removed from our marketing, email legal@peanutgallery.live.
How do I contact a human?
support@peanutgallery.live for bug reports, or open a GitHub issue.
