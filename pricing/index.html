§ 02 · Never shipped
Documented for posterity

Plus did not ship. The plan was a flat-rate $8/month hosted backend — pay us, paste one license key, get 16 hours/week of transcription and persona reactions, no API keys to juggle. The pricing was set at break-even with a thin infra margin (full economics in the subscription architecture doc). It was designed as an accessibility tier for non-technical users, not as a profit center.

It never went live. Manu Games LLC unpublished the Chrome Web Store listing on 2026-05-02 before the subscription opened. There is nothing to buy here. The architecture, weekly-cap math, license-key flow, and Stripe integration are all documented below for anyone who wants to fork the source and build their own hosted tier — at their own legal concern.

If you are forking and standing up your own hosted backend: you own the operator side completely. You handle Stripe / billing / refunds / sales tax / ToS / DMCA / abuse policy / acceptable-use enforcement / provider AUP flow-down / defamation exposure for sessions you produce. The economics below were calculated against Manu Games' contracts and free-tier credits; your numbers will differ.

How it would have worked, end to end

The Plus design is preserved below in past tense as an architectural reference for forks. No account system. No password. No OAuth. Plus uses a license key over email — the same pattern as a coupon code — because that's the lightest-weight way to identify a subscription without introducing a whole login surface.

Subscribe from the side panel

Open the settings gear → pick Plus on the backend toggle → click Subscribe. You'll be asked for one thing: your email.

2

Pay on Stripe

A Stripe Checkout page opens. $8/month. No trial add-on, no upsell. We never see your card; Stripe handles it.

3

Key arrives, paste, go

§ 03 · FAQ
Is any feature Plus-only?
No. Plus does not unlock anything. It's a way to pay for compute without juggling keys. Everything in the extension is free and MIT-licensed — fork the repo, it's all there.
What happens if I cancel mid-month?
Your subscription runs to the end of the current billing period, then stops. You keep using Plus for the days you paid for. The license key stops validating at period end; you can swap the drawer back to My keys or Demo with zero data loss.
Can I upgrade from BYOK to Plus later? Or the other way around?
Yes, seamlessly. The settings drawer has three modes — Demo, My keys, and Plus — and you switch between them with a single click. Your saved keys and your Plus subscription coexist. You can even run a session on Plus and swap mid-session to your own keys if you want to preserve the weekly cap for later.
What if I share my key with a friend?
The cap is per-subscription, not per-device — so if two of you share a key, you share the 16 hours. No device enforcement, no DRM, no "authorized devices" list. This is the honor system, priced so splitting it with a roommate isn't worth the friction.
Is my audio stored anywhere?
No. Audio is streamed to Deepgram live, transcripts fan out to Anthropic + xAI live, everything is held in memory for the duration of the session and discarded when you click Stop. No logs, no recordings, no analytics inside the extension. The privacy policy covers the full picture.
Why 16 hours and not unlimited?
Because uncapped means we eventually lose money on a single power user and have to raise the price on everyone. 16 h/week ≈ 2 h/day every day of the week — well above the median YouTube habit — with headroom for a couple long podcasts on a weekend. When upstream API costs drop, the cap goes up or the price goes down.
What models power Plus?
Same as BYOK: Deepgram Nova-3 for real-time transcription, Claude Haiku for the fact-checker + comedy writer, xAI Grok 4.1 Fast for the troll + sound-effects guy. No model downgrades for subscribers.
What about business use / teams?
There's no team plan today. One person, one subscription, any number of their own devices. If a team license makes sense later, it'll be announced — and it'll honor the "never raise the individual price" commitment regardless.
Is this AI-generated content safe to show at work?
Sometimes. The Troll is designed to dunk and The Bit comic is designed to joke — edit the personas (the extension supports sideloading your own pack) if you want a more SFW room. Self-host the backend, write a pack of four Jason Calacanis clones, swap in at runtime. The Operator's Manual covers this.
Are these the real people?
No. The packs are inspired by public figures from The Morning Crew and This Week in Startups — unofficial, unauthorized fan commentary. We have no license, no endorsement, no affiliation. The AI is prompted to clarify it's a fan parody if a user asks whether it's the real person. Full legal framing in the Terms of Service. If you are one of these public figures (or represent one) and want your name removed, email legal@peanutgallery.live — we respond promptly and in good faith.
What if Plus shuts down?
BYOK is unaffected — the extension works forever because you own the provider accounts. If we ever retired the hosted backend, we'd email every Plus subscriber, refund unused time, and leave the extension fully functional on BYOK and self-host. Open source is the insurance policy.
How do I contact a human?
support@peanutgallery.live for billing, refunds, lost keys, bug reports. For code-level issues, open a GitHub issue.
