Three principles.

§ 02 · Why Plus exists
The whole reason there's a subscription tier

Accessibility, not profit.

BYOK is and stays free forever. Plus exists so non-technical viewers can reach the same product without juggling four API consoles. If margins improve, we drive the price down, not the cap up.

Same models, no downgrades.

Plus runs the exact stack BYOK runs — Deepgram Nova-3, Claude Haiku, Grok 4.1 Fast. No paywalled models, no quality split between paying and bringing-keys.

16 hours covers the cap.

That's two long-form podcasts a day. Subscribers who hit it can flip to BYOK mid-week without losing either configuration. Heavy users have always been BYOK-best.

FAQ.

§ 04 · Q & A

Why $8?

Casual usage at 4–8 h/week costs us about $4.80–$9.60 in API spend. $8 sits roughly at break-even for that load with a thin margin for infrastructure. The math is fully written up in the subscription architecture doc.

What if I hit the 16 h cap?

The side panel surfaces a soft "cap reached, resets in N days" message. You can either wait for the Monday reset or flip the same session to BYOK and keep going on your own keys. Your subscription doesn't get charged twice.

How do I cancel?

Side panel → Plus → Manage. We email you a one-time Stripe Customer Portal link. One click in Stripe's hosted UI cancels at end-of-billing-period. The key is revoked at the period end; refunds on request via support.

What happens to my data?

Same answer as BYOK. Audio is streamed and discarded — never written to disk. Transcript stays in RAM and is gone at session end. The only persistent records are: your license key, your email (for recovery + cancel), and a per-week usage counter (just milliseconds). No transcripts, no IP logging, no advertising. Full privacy policy.

Is there a free trial?

Every install gets a one-off, lifetime 15-minute trial of the hosted backend. After that you bring your own keys (free forever) or subscribe to Plus.

Lost my key?

Side panel → Plus → Email me my key. We email the active key tied to the email on your subscription. We can't enumerate (the response is identical whether or not the email is on file) so check the spelling.
