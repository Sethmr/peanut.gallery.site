Peanut Gallery Plus · cancellation

Cancel your
subscription

Enter the email address on your Plus account. We'll send a one-tap link to Stripe's hosted customer portal where you can cancel.

No account dashboard, no password — just email. Your license key stays active through the end of the billing period you've already paid for.

What happens next

  1. If the email matches an active subscription, Stripe emails you a magic link in under a minute.
  2. Click the link — it opens the Stripe-hosted customer portal.
  3. Hit "Cancel subscription" on that page. Stripe confirms; our backend processes the webhook; your Plus access ends at the close of the current billing period.
  4. If the email doesn't match an active subscription, you'll still get a success confirmation here (we don't leak which emails are on file) — but no email will arrive.

Stuck? Email support@peanutgallery.live with the card's last four digits and we'll cancel manually.
