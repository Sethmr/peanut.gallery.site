We've emailed your license key to the address you used at checkout. It usually lands within a minute. Search the inbox for "Peanut Gallery Plus" if you don't see it.

  1. Open the email from subscriptions@peanutgallery.live with subject "Welcome to Peanut Gallery Plus." Copy the license key (format: pg-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx).
  2. Open the side panel on any YouTube tab. Click the gear at the bottom → Backend & keys → pick the Plus tab.
  3. Paste the key into the Subscription key field. The weekly progress bar appears once the key validates.
  4. Press Start on a video. The booth fires within 30 seconds. Your weekly cap counts down in the side panel.

Lost the email? Side panel → Plus → Email me my key. Need to cancel? Same place → Manage. Both go through Stripe's hosted Customer Portal.
