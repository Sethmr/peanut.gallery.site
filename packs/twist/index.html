Molly Wood keeps the timeline honest. Jason calls the bit. Lon drops the cue. Alex lands the joke. Four personas built to ride along on any This Week in Startups episode — the pack that started the bounty.

The bounty pack.

§ 01 · Why TWiST
For the All-In-adjacent VC podcast crowd

Peanut Gallery exists because Jason Calacanis put a $5K bounty on building a live AI sidebar for TWiST. The TWiST pack is the answer — four personas modeled on the actual show's energy: a producer who corrects the dates, a host who heckles his own guests, an editor who scores the segment, and a comedy writer who lands the callback.

Pin the peanut to your toolbar, open @thisweekin in a tab, click Start. The booth fires within 30 seconds. Every reaction is a wire-service entry — timestamped, role-tagged, queued in the side panel as the show plays.

§ 05 · Q & A

Does this work on the All-In Podcast too?

Sort of — the TWiST pack is calibrated for the TWiST cadence (one-host-plus-guest pitch energy). All-In's four-besties dynamic is on the v2.x roadmap as a dedicated pack. In the meantime the TWiST pack works on All-In with about 70% of the relevance.

Will it actually fact-check Jason?

Yes. Molly's slot runs against xAI Grok Live Search and is tuned to flag valuations, funding rounds, and date claims. The producer prompt explicitly says “don't pile on; flag the load-bearing facts.”

Is this affiliated with Jason, Inside.com, or LAUNCH?

No. Peanut Gallery is an independent open-source project that was built to compete for the publicly announced TWiST $5K bounty. The personas are inspired by the show's archetypes — they don't speak as the actual hosts and the system prompts say so explicitly.

Where does the bounty stand?

The bounty is open. Submission link, scoring criteria, and Seth's pitch live in the README on GitHub. If you're @jason and you're reading this — you can email seth@manugames.com.

Can I author my own pack for [my favorite podcast]?

Yes. The pack authoring guide walks through the four archetype slots, the persona-prompt contract, and how to register a new pack. About a day of work for a competent writer.
