Morning Crew.

Four AI booth personas — the Producer, the Heckler, the Sound Guy, and the Joke Writer — sit next to any YouTube clip and file their reactions in real time. Late-night newsroom energy in your side panel, no studio required.

The booth, ported.

§ 01 · The pack
For long-form interview, podcast, and live-show fans

The Morning Crew pack is the original lineup that shipped with Peanut Gallery v1.0 and stayed the default ever since. It's built for long-form video — the segments where a producer keeps the timeline honest, a heckler dunks on the guest, the sound guy drops cues, and the joke writer punches up a callback once an hour.

Pin the peanut to your toolbar, open any YouTube clip with audio, click Start in the side panel. Audio is captured silently from the tab — no screen-share picker, no playback interference. The four personas file as a wire-service feed: every reaction stamped with a 24-hour timestamp and a role tag (FACT, DUNK, CUE, BIT).

The cast.

§ 02 · Four slots
Each persona owns one archetype
Slot 01 · Fact
The Producer
Sidekick / fact-checker foil
Keeps the booth tied to ground truth — name spellings, timeline corrections, the date-said-vs-actual-date check. Trolly-EP voice, eye-roll-pivot register, no profanity. Four-tier fact-check taxonomy: CONFIRMS / CONTRADICTS / COMPLICATES / THIN.
Claude Haiku · xAI Live Search
Slot 02 · Dunk
The Heckler
Composite call-in voice
Seven sub-voices, single voice per turn (bureaucratic deadpan / landmine question / screech / confident nonsense / grievance spiral / regional simile / earnest prank). Targets only willing public performers of power, authority, virtue, or glamour — never private people, grief, or vulnerable targets.
xAI Grok 4.1 Fast
Slot 03 · Cue
The Sound Guy
Drops, zingers, impressions
Five output modes (SFX drop / one-clause zinger / in-character impression / silence / micro-monologue), six named B-mode sentence shapes, twelve trolly-edge target categories. Bone-dry one-clause delivery. Defaults to silence when in doubt.
xAI Grok 4.1 Fast
Slot 04 · Bit
The Joke Writer
Setups + punchlines + signature laugh
Ten named tactical moves (Q&A wordplay, bar shaggy, marriage one-liner, doctor / aging, animal absurd, bathroom function, mid-word laugh-break, ≤7-word interjection, self-deprecation pivot). Closes every response on a laugh tag, plug, or callback — never on a straight sentence.

§ 05 · Q & A

Does this work on any YouTube channel?

Yes — anywhere a YouTube tab is playing audio, the side panel can listen. Interviews, podcasts, livestreams, late-night clips, sports breakdowns — all work the same.

Can it do shows that aren't on YouTube?

Only if the audio is playing in a Chrome tab. The extension can't hear native macOS or Windows audio outside the browser. Workaround: most podcasts and shows hit YouTube within hours.

Will The Producer actually fact-check the host?

Yes. The producer slot runs against xAI Grok Live Search and is tuned to flag dates, name spellings, and resolved-on-the-record details. He's friendlier than a typical fact-checker — calibrated for an entertainment context, not a debate moderator.

Can I swap in different characters?

Yes — pack swap is in the operator's manual. Today's options are Morning Crew (default) and TWiST. Authoring your own pack is documented in the pack authoring guide.

Are the personas based on specific real people?

No. The four personas are inspired by morning-radio shock-jock-show archetypes — the producer, the heckler, the sound-effects sideman, the joke writer — not by any specific real-world host, producer, or crew member. The kernels are written so the model never claims to be a real person and identifies as an AI fan-parody if asked.
