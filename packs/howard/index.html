The booth, ported.

§ 01 · The pack
For Stern Show fans who watch on YouTube

The Howard pack is the original lineup that shipped with Peanut Gallery v1.0 and stayed the default ever since. It's built for long-form Stern interviews — the segments where a producer keeps the timeline honest, a troll heckles the guest, the sound guy drops cues, and the comedy writer punches up a callback once an hour.

Pin the peanut to your toolbar, open any Stern clip on YouTube, click Start in the side panel. Audio is captured silently from the tab — no screen-share picker, no playback interference. The four personas file as a wire-service feed: every reaction stamped with a 24-hour timestamp and a role tag (FACT, DUNK, CUE, BIT).

§ 02 · Four slots
Each persona owns one archetype
Slot 01 · Fact
Baba Booey
The producer / fact-checker
Keeps the booth tied to ground truth — name spellings, timeline corrections, the date-Howard-said-vs-actual-date check. Quiet most of the segment, then drops a [HEADS UP] when something needs flagging.
Claude Haiku · Grok Live Search
Slot 02 · Dunk
The Troll
The cynical co-host
Heckles the guest, calls out the bit, refuses to be impressed. Fires when the timing pops — never on a content-free tail. Persona prompt is the Wack-Pack-shaped foil to whoever's on the show that day.
xAI Grok 4.1 Fast
Slot 03 · Cue
Fred
The sound effects guy
Drops cues. Boos, applause, sad trombones, a record scratch when a guest contradicts themselves. Sound is text-only on the page — every cue is a one-liner like “[BOO] — that's the third time he's said ‘synergy’.”
xAI Grok 4.1 Fast
Slot 04 · Bit
Jackie
The comedy writer
Once-an-hour punch-up. Lands a joke that re-frames the segment so far. Opens a callback instead of closing one. The slow burn of the four — fewer fires, higher upvote rate.

§ 05 · Q & A

Does this work on Howard's official YouTube channel?

Yes — anywhere a YouTube tab is playing audio, the side panel can listen. Howard's official channel, the Wack Pack uploads, fan-clip channels, full episode rips — all work the same.

Can it do the SiriusXM shows?

Only if the audio is playing in a Chrome tab. The extension can't hear native macOS or Windows audio outside the browser. Workaround: a lot of Stern segments hit YouTube within hours.

Will Baba Booey actually fact-check Howard?

Yes. The producer slot runs against xAI Grok Live Search and is tuned to flag dates, name spellings, and resolved-on-the-record details. He's friendlier than a typical fact-checker — calibrated for an entertainment context, not a debate moderator.

Can I swap in different characters?

Yes — pack swap is in the operator's manual. Today's options are Howard (default) and TWiST. Authoring your own pack is documented in the pack authoring guide.

Is this affiliated with Howard, SiriusXM, or any cast member?

No. Peanut Gallery is an independent open-source project. The personas are inspired by archetypes from a Stern-style booth — they don't claim to be the actual cast members and the prompts are written so the model never speaks as them.
